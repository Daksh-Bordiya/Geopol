# **Overview**

**Reliance Industries** and **Rolls-Royce** have signed a **strategic intent agreement** (have agreed in principle) to co-develop engines for **India’s 5th-generation AMCA (Mark 2)** fighter jet. The partnership includes end-to-end design, testing, and manufacturing of the jet engine along with complete ToT (Transfer of Technology) and full/joint IP ownership. It is yet to be approved by the associated government bodies.. 

## Strategic Impact

#### Until recently, France's **Safran** was seen as the favorite to **win** **this** ₹**61,000 crore AMCA engine deal** because if we trace back in time France served as a strategic defence partner of India.. Recently India bought **36** Rafale jet and Indian Navy is buying **26** Naval Rafales and in all these jets the engine used is of Safran.. Hence it makes it more favourable for India to give this deal to Safran as they also promised a 10-year development cycle..

But the French were doing this deal on their own terms of :

- **Incomplete technology transfer**  
- **Strict export restriction**  
- **Restricted IP ownership**  
- **India dependent of France for maintenance of these engines**
- **Higher Prices**

Hence, This new development of **Reliance Industries** and **Rolls-Royce** would act as a leverage for doing negotiations with **Safran** as well as if they continue ahead with this **JV** (Joint Venture) of **Reliance Industries** and **Rolls-Royce**, it would help India to develop an eco-system of production of jet engines which are **100%** "**Made In India**"  with complete IP and ownership.. which would help in future indian jet programs like:           **Tejas MK-2**, **IMRH** (Indian Multi-Role Helicopter), **RTA** (Regional Transport Aircraft) and etc and could even help in long term to export it's own fighter jet's to the world...
